\section*{Abstract.}

The Patitas Felices project involves developing a web-based system for managing and administering a veterinary clinic. Its purpose is to improve the organization of clinic activities and facilitate the management of information about pet owners and their pets. The system aims to reduce manual processes, optimize service time, and maintain organized, accessible records of veterinary services.

The platform will include six main modules: owners and pets, scheduling and availability, appointments and medical records, vaccinations and follow-ups, supplies and inventory, and a portal and reports. It will also support different user roles—administrator, receptionist, veterinarian, and pet owner—each with specific functions and permissions. The system is developed using technologies such as PHP, Laravel, SQLite, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, which enable the creation of a well-organized, functional web application suited to the clinic's needs.

In conclusion, Patitas Felices aims to provide a technological solution that simplifies veterinary clinic administration, improves information management, and helps deliver more organized and efficient care to pets and their owners.
